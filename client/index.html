Real parts break off

Your stuff. Real parts. Real fights.

Snap a photo of anything in your home. Aholo Lux3D builds it in 3D and splits it into the parts it is really made of. Then it fights. Hard hits knock those parts right off.

Free. Plays in the browser. Phones become controllers.

How it works

Four steps from your junk drawer to the arena.

  1. Snap it

    Scan the QR code with your phone and take a photo of one object. A mug, a shoe, a toy, a pan.

  2. Lux3D builds it

    Aholo Lux3D turns the photo into a 3D model in about two minutes, then splits it into its real parts like a handle, a lid or a sole.

  3. AI makes the card

    An AI agent looks at the photo and works out the material, real size and weight. That decides the stats, the special move and the trash talk.

  4. Battle

    Two to four fighters drop into the arena. Real physics decides every hit. Last one standing wins.

Where card power comes from

Nothing is random. Every number comes from the real object.

The four stats
PowerHow hard it hits. Dense and heavy things like cast iron hit hardest.
SpeedHow fast it moves and how fast its energy refills. Light things are quick.
ArmorHow much damage it shrugs off. Ceramic and metal are tough. Foam and paper are not.
BalanceHow well it stays upright. Wide flat bases balance well. Tall thin things tip over.

Stats always add up to 24, so every card is fair. A pan trades speed for power. A rubber duck trades power for speed. The purple number on a card is its level. Levels add health, so a card you fight with often gets stronger.

Five elements

The AI picks an element from what your object is made of. Each one has its own hit effects and one perk.

🔥FireWood, candles, spicy things

Special move sets the target on fire for 2 seconds.

💧WaterGlass, rubber, bottles

Gets 1 energy back every time it is hit.

⚡LightningMetal, batteries, gadgets

Special move hits 25 percent harder.

🌪️AirFabric, foam, paper, shoes

Dodge costs 2 energy and jumps go higher.

🪨EarthCeramic, stone, clay, plants

Takes 15 percent less damage.

Battle rules

Fighters move on their own. You choose when to spend energy. Pause any time with P or the pause button.

Energy

Each fighter has 10 energy. It refills every second. Quick fighters refill faster.

  • 4 Special move
  • 3 Dodge
  • 2 Jump
Parts and health

Health is spread across the real parts. The body holds 40 percent. The rest is split by size.

When a part runs out it breaks off and flies away as debris. Debris can hit other fighters too.

If the body breaks, the fighter falls apart.

How to win

Shatter the other fighter, or knock it off the edge.

After 45 seconds the arena starts to shrink. At 95 seconds the fighter with the most health wins.

Phone players get Attack, Jump and Dodge buttons. Nobody pressing? The AI plays.

Level up and climb

Your first pick in the deck is your leader. Trophies follow the leader.

Trophies

Your leader wins: +30. Your leader loses: −12. Trophies unlock new arenas.

Card levels

Every fighter in a match earns XP. The winner earns double. Each level adds 5 percent health, up to level 10.
